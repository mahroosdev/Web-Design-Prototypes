Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Served with Love

At Savora, we believe dining is more than a meal — it's an experience. Founded by Chef Adrienne Moreau, our kitchen blends classic French technique with bold, seasonal ingredients sourced from local farms and artisan producers.

Every dish on our menu reflects a commitment to craft, from the hand-rolled pasta to the wood-fired proteins that define our signature style. We invite you to slow down, savor, and discover flavors that surprise and delight.
