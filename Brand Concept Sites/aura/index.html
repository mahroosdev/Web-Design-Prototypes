Our Origin

Born from a
single obsession.

What if coffee could taste the way morning light feels on a quiet winter day?

We traveled 47,000 kilometers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Japan before we understood what we were searching for — not a flavor, but a feeling. A moment of absolute stillness in a cup.

AURA was built on the belief that the world's finest coffee should reach those who truly seek it, without compromise, without shortcut, and without apology.

Mads Eriksen
Founder & Head Roaster
The Process

From seed
to ceremony.

Stage 01
Discovery &
Farm Selection

Our sourcing team visits 40+ farms each season, tasting and evaluating directly — no brokers, no intermediaries. Only farms with verifiable sustainable practices.

Stage 02
Harvest &
Processing

Cherries are handpicked at peak ripeness. Each lot is processed — washed, natural, or honey — according to what the terroir demands, never convenience.

Stage 03
Green Coffee
Import

Shipped in GrainPro sacks to preserve moisture and flavor. Every container is cup-scored on arrival at our Copenhagen roastery before any batch proceeds.

Stage 04
Small-Batch
Roasting

Each roast is developed by hand on a 12kg Loring drum. Profiles crafted to honor the unique character of each lot — never to mask, always to reveal.

Stage 05
Quality
Cupping

Every batch is cupped blind against our reference standard. If it doesn't score 87+ on the SCA scale, it does not leave the studio. No exceptions, ever.

Stage 06
Delivery &
Your Ritual

Nitrogen-flushed, sealed, dispatched within 24 hours. Carbon-neutral. It arrives ready for its moment — your morning, your ceremony, your stillness.
